How these estimates are made, and their limits

Modelled, not observed. These are estimates from survey-based microdata, not counts of real homes or bills. Seat estimates are uncertain: for a typical constituency shown, the standard error is about 38% of the estimate, and the ranking of the largest seats is steadier (a resample keeps 17 of the top 20). These measures, like the 90% range for England (£350m–£470m), come from resampling the survey households only; they leave out calibration and modelling error, so the true uncertainty is larger. They follow the pattern of council tax band H homes closely (rank correlation 0.77), and the 110 seats shown include all of the 20 constituencies with the most homes over £2m.

Data. Microcosm UK 2024-25 local-area release (cut microcosm-uk-2024-25-dense-20261019T000432Z-ee5c2dbe, 150,000 households, SHA-256 8e10e130d026…), PolicyEngine's synthetic microdata built from the Family Resources Survey and other UK Data Service surveys and calibrated to official local statistics. Each owner's home takes its value from its constituency's Land Registry sale prices at the household's rank among owners in its region, and the release is calibrated to Land Registry counts of owner-occupied homes over £1m, £1.5m and £2m by region and local authority. policyengine 6.10.0 loads this release for constituency results, with policyengine-uk 2.132.6. Its certificate covers the national release built from the same data, not constituency results from this local-area release, so these seat figures are not certified. Revenue applies the surcharge as policyengine-uk 2.132.6 defines it to each household's modelled home value. The microdata are licensed and not public.

Policy. England only, from April 2028, owners liable: £2,500 a year for homes worth £2m–£2.5m, £3,500 for £2.5m–£3.5m, £5,000 for £3.5m–£5m and £7,500 above £5m, at April 2026 values. policyengine-uk uprates 2024 values to 2026 by GDP per capita (+7.4%); house prices (+5.3%) would put fewer homes over each threshold. CPI uprating of the charges from 2029-30 is not modelled. Revenue is static: no behaviour, appeals, non-payment or deferral.

Coverage. Only owner-occupied main residences are modelled, so second homes and let or company-owned homes are missing. Their share is uncertain: the OBR puts non-owner-occupied homes at about 40% of liable properties (from stamp duty data), while MHCLG's Market Value Survey, cited in the May 2026 consultation, found over 90% of homes worth £2m or more owner occupied in 2024.

Privacy. The survey data are licensed from the UK Data Service. To protect respondents, a constituency is reported only if at least 10 survey households above £2m sit behind its estimate, and every estimate from the survey microdata carries keyed noise: each survey household's weight is multiplied by a fixed secret factor between 8% and 12% away from 1. That typically moves a constituency's estimate by a few per cent, more where one household carries much of it.

Maps. The hex map gives every constituency the same size, so small urban seats stay visible; the geographic map shows the 2024 constituency boundaries (contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2024), simplified to about 250 m.
